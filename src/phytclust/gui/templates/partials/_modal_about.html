About PhytClust

PhytClust {{ phytclust_version }} partitions the leaves of a rooted phylogenetic tree into monophyletic clusters by dynamic programming, and selects the number of clusters from prominence-based peaks of a clustering-validity score.

If you use PhytClust, please cite:
